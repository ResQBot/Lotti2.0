# Power Delivery
The Power delivery is split into two section, one section gets the two battery inputs, merges them to a stable 24V for further use and also secures two individual 24V lines for driving the Motors. A lot of measurements also happen here.

The other part is the Conversion to regulate the 24V for the Motors to 12V for some sensores, the STM32 and also the Control PC and optional 5V for other things.
